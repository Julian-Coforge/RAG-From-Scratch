# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose and scope

Northbridge Systems reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses they incur while performing authorised work. This policy applies to expenses paid personally and purchases made on a corporate card. Reconcile corporate card transactions in the expense portal so the charges can be assigned to the correct cost centre. Contractors follow the billing terms in their engagement agreements and should confirm any reimbursable purchases with their Northbridge contact before committing funds. This version applies to expenses incurred from **1 January through 30 September 2026**, inclusive.

## Approval and eligible expenses

Obtain written manager approval before booking an overnight trip or making an individual purchase over **$250 USD** or its local currency equivalent. Purchases over **$1,000 USD** also require written Finance approval. Attach both approvals when applicable and treat related items bought together as one purchase for approval purposes. Use the company booking portal where it is available. Economy airfare, standard rail class, and reasonably priced accommodation are eligible when a trip is necessary for business. Include a short explanation if a change to a confirmed booking results in extra costs.

Ordinary commuting, personal purchases, and fines are ineligible. A taxi or rideshare between business locations can be claimed when the employee records the start and end points and explains why the journey was needed. Home office equipment is eligible only when Information Technology confirms the item is needed and the employee’s manager approves the purchase in writing before the order is placed.

## Meals and incidentals

During approved overnight business travel, employees may claim actual meal costs up to **$55 USD per person per calendar day**, including tax and tip. The cap also applies on the first and last days of travel. It cannot be carried forward or averaged across days. A meal included in a hotel rate, conference fee, or company booking may not be claimed separately. Employees should provide a brief business purpose and the names of attendees for a hosted client meal. Client meals require prior written manager approval and are reviewed separately from the employee’s daily travel meal limit.

Alcohol is ineligible on individual travel meal claims. For client hospitality that includes alcohol, obtain written approval from Finance before the event. When a receipt includes personal items, identify those items and exclude them from the claim total.

## Receipts and submission

Submit claims in the expense portal within **45 calendar days** of the transaction date. Enter the merchant, transaction date, amount, currency, cost centre, and business purpose for each item. An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **$50 USD or more**, or its local currency equivalent. A bank statement or card receipt alone does not show what was purchased. If the merchant cannot replace a missing receipt, submit a missing-receipt declaration explaining the purchase and attach any available proof of payment. Finance decides whether the declaration is sufficient.

Enter foreign currency expenses in the currency shown on the receipt. Where a personal card charge shows the settled amount in the employee’s reimbursement currency, attach that entry to the claim. For cash purchases and card transactions without an available settled amount, Finance uses the expense portal’s rate for the transaction date.

## Review, payment and corrections

The manager confirms the business purpose and the cost centre. Finance reviews receipts, approvals, policy limits, and duplicate entries. Finance returns incomplete claims through the portal with a request for the missing information. Approved personal claims are paid in the next scheduled reimbursement run. If a merchant issues a refund after submission, record it against the original claim and notify Finance within five business days of receiving it. Submit a late claim with an explanation and manager endorsement. Finance records the decision and will reimburse a late claim only if it approves an exception.
