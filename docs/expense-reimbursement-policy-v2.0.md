# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose and scope

Northbridge Systems reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses they incur while performing authorised work. This policy applies to personally paid expenses and purchases made on a corporate card. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the expense portal even though the employee is not seeking repayment. Contractors should follow the billing and expense terms in their engagement agreements. This version applies to expenses incurred on or after **1 October 2026**. Use the transaction date printed on the receipt to determine which version applies.

## Approval and eligible expenses

Obtain written manager approval before booking an overnight trip or making an individual purchase over **$250 USD** or its local currency equivalent. Obtain written Finance approval as well for any individual purchase over **$1,000 USD**. Attach approvals to the claim. Do not split a purchase across transactions to avoid an approval threshold. Use the company booking portal to book travel where it is available. Economy airfare, standard rail class, and reasonably priced accommodation are eligible when required for business. Add a short explanation if changing a booking results in extra costs.

Ordinary commuting, personal purchases, and fines are ineligible. A local taxi or rideshare between business locations can be claimed if the journey is necessary for work and the start and end points are recorded. Home office equipment is eligible only when Information Technology confirms the item is needed and the employee’s manager approves the purchase in writing before the order is placed.

## Meals and incidentals

During approved overnight business travel, employees may claim actual meal costs up to **$70 USD per person per calendar day**, including tax and tip. The cap also applies on the first and last days of travel. It cannot be carried forward or averaged across days. A meal included in a hotel rate, conference fee, or company booking may not be claimed separately. Employees should provide a brief business purpose and the names of attendees for a hosted client meal. Client meals require prior written manager approval and are reviewed separately from the employee’s daily travel meal limit.

Alcohol is ineligible on individual travel meal claims. A client hospitality claim that includes alcohol requires explicit written approval from Finance before the event. Employees should identify personal items on a mixed receipt and subtract them before submitting the amount claimed.

## Receipts and submission

Submit claims in the expense portal within **30 calendar days** of the transaction date. Enter the merchant, transaction date, amount, currency, cost centre, and business purpose for each item. An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **$25 USD or more**, or its local currency equivalent. A bank statement or card receipt alone does not show what was purchased. If the merchant cannot replace a missing receipt, submit a missing-receipt declaration explaining the purchase and attach any available proof of payment. Finance decides whether the declaration is sufficient.

For foreign currency expenses, enter the amount and currency shown on the receipt. Where the employee paid by personal card, attach the card statement entry if it shows the settled amount in the employee’s reimbursement currency. If that entry is unavailable or the item was paid in cash, Finance will apply the expense portal’s rate for the transaction date.

## Review, payment and corrections

The manager confirms the business purpose and the cost centre. Finance verifies documentation, policy limits, and duplicate entries. Finance returns incomplete claims through the portal and tells the employee which information to supply. Approved personal claims are paid in the next scheduled reimbursement run. If a merchant issues a refund after submission, record it against the original claim and notify Finance within five business days of receiving it. Submit a late claim with an explanation and manager endorsement. Finance records the decision and will reimburse a late claim only if it approves an exception.
