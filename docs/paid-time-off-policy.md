# Paid Time Off Policy

## Purpose and scope

Northbridge Systems provides paid time off (PTO) for holidays, rest, and personal commitments. This policy applies to permanent full-time and part-time employees. The allowances below are the standard company benefit. People Operations applies any higher entitlement or different treatment required by an employee’s local employment terms. Sick leave, parental leave, and other separately administered leave types are handled by People Operations. Contact that team if you are unsure how an absence should be recorded.

## Entitlement and accrual

The PTO year runs from **1 January to 31 December**. Full-time employees on the standard schedule accrue **24 days per year**, credited at two days at the end of each completed month. Employees who work part time accrue a proportionate allowance based on their contracted hours. Payroll calculates partial months for new starters and departing employees using the fraction of the calendar month they were employed. Leave is stored in hours in the HR portal so that a day off can be charged according to the employee’s scheduled hours for that day.

Company-observed public holidays are recorded separately and do not reduce the employee’s PTO balance. A day on which the employee is not scheduled to work also does not reduce their balance. Employees can take PTO for a full scheduled day or half of a scheduled day. Employees should contact People Operations if the portal shows a balance that differs from their pay or work schedule records.

## Requests and approvals

Submit PTO requests in the HR portal and confirm that the dates and hours are correct. For absences of one to four consecutive scheduled working days, submit the request at least **five business days** before the first day of leave. For five or more consecutive scheduled working days, give at least **15 business days’ notice**. The manager reviews coverage, previously approved absences, and the employee’s available balance before approving the request. Employees should wait for approval before making non-refundable bookings.

If several requests would leave a team without adequate coverage, the manager should consider when the requests were submitted and any commitments already made to the employees involved. If a request is declined, the manager must explain the coverage constraint and offer an opportunity to discuss other dates. For an unexpected absence that may need to be recorded as PTO, notify the manager as soon as practicable and enter the request when able. The manager may approve shorter notice for an urgent personal circumstance.

## Carryover and advance use

Employees on standard terms may carry over up to **five days of unused PTO** into the following year. Carried-over time must be used by **31 March** and is applied before the new year’s accrual. Any remaining carried-over time expires after that date unless People Operations confirms an exception or a local entitlement requires different treatment. Employees who want an exception because business needs prevented them from taking approved time should ask their manager and People Operations before the carryover deadline.

Employees may request to use up to two days of PTO before those days are earned. Both the manager and People Operations must approve the request in writing. The HR portal reflects the advance against future accrual. Employees should check their balance before submitting subsequent requests.

## Changes, handovers and records

Employees should update the original portal request if approved dates change, then confirm the revised approval with their manager. Before an absence, employees should hand over time-sensitive work, set an out-of-office message, and identify a contact for urgent matters. Employees are not expected to monitor work during approved PTO. The HR portal is the official record of approvals, accruals, and carryover balances. People Operations resolves discrepancies and confirms how any outstanding balance is handled when an employee leaves.
