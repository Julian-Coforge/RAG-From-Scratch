# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose and scope

Northbridge Systems supports remote work where it is compatible with customer commitments, team coverage, and secure handling of company information. This policy applies to permanent full-time and part-time employees whose role is designated as hybrid or fully remote in the HR portal. Office-based employees may request an occasional remote day through their manager. Contractors follow the location terms in their agreements and should direct any requests to their Northbridge engagement lead.

## Hybrid schedules and availability

Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely up to **two days per working week**, subject to their manager’s approval. A working week runs Monday through Friday. An unused remote day expires at the end of that week. A day spent at a customer site counts as on-site work. Approved leave is recorded separately and does not use the remote work allowance.

Employees must enter their proposed remote days in the HR portal by noon local time on the preceding Thursday. Managers should respond by the end of Friday. A recurring schedule can be approved for up to one quarter, after which the manager reviews it with the employee. Employees must keep their calendars current and be reachable from **10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their assigned office time zone**, except during breaks, meetings, or approved time away. Fully remote employees must agree on their coverage hours with their manager in writing.

## Work locations and exceptions

Employees may work from their registered home address or another private location within their country of employment. Employees planning to use a different domestic location for more than five consecutive working days must notify their manager and People Operations before the arrangement begins. The employee must provide the location and dates. Work from another country requires written approval from People Operations and Information Security before travel is booked. Submit requests at least **15 business days** before departure and wait for a written decision before working from the proposed location.

A manager may approve a same-day change to an existing hybrid schedule for a transport disruption or urgent personal circumstance. The employee should contact the manager before the normal start of the workday, then record the approved change in the HR portal. Requests for ongoing workplace adjustments go directly to People Operations, which will discuss the arrangement with the employee and relevant manager.

## Equipment and information security

Use a company-managed device, enable multi-factor authentication, and connect to the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside a Northbridge office or private home network. Employees must prevent other people from viewing confidential material or overhearing sensitive conversations. Lock the screen whenever stepping away, even at home. Company records must be stored in approved systems. Personal email accounts, personal cloud storage, and shared household devices may not be used for company work.

Information Technology supplies a laptop and standard peripherals. Employees who need additional equipment should open an IT service desk request before purchasing it. Reimbursement eligibility and approval requirements are set out in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-204). Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk immediately, even if the device is locked or the employee expects to recover it.

## Recordkeeping and review

Employees are responsible for keeping approved schedules and location changes current in the HR portal. Managers review team coverage each quarter and document any schedule changes there. People Operations maintains the policy and the record of approved exceptions. Employees should raise questions about eligibility, exceptions, or conflicting scheduling instructions with People Operations. If a manager changes an approved recurring schedule, they should tell the employee which dates are affected and allow reasonable notice when operational needs permit.
